Joshua Grey

Peruvian comic book artist and graphic storyteller, awarded an Honourable Mention in the 4th National Graphic Narrative Competition. Read the Ministry of Culture feature ↗

His comics explore the narrative possibilities of Indian ink, charcoal, and colour experimentation, with a particular interest in dark atmospheres and stories that drift between the everyday and the uncanny.

Available for editorial collaborations, comic projects, and graphic storytelling commissions.
